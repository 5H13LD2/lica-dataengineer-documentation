Brand: Cooper | Section: Coverage – Standard Coverage
If a Cooper branded radial replacement tire becomes unserviceable due to an eligible adjustable condition during the first 2/32 inch (1.6 millimeters) of tread wear, the tire will be replaced with an equivalent new Cooper tire free of charge.
If the tread wear exceeds 2/32 inch (1.6 millimeters), a replacement charge will apply in order to obtain a replacement Cooper tire.

Brand: Cooper | Section: Eligibility – Proof of Purchase and Ownership
To qualify for Cooper warranty replacement, the customer must be the original owner of the tire and must present valid proof of purchase at the time of the replacement request.

Brand: Cooper | Section: Replacement Cost Calculation
When a replacement charge applies, the cost is calculated by multiplying the dealer’s current selling price of the replacement Cooper tire by the percentage of original tread depth that has been worn from the tire.

Brand: Cooper | Section: Customer Costs and Additional Charges
Under the Cooper warranty, the customer is responsible for paying mounting, balancing, and any other additional charges.
This includes applicable taxes and any price difference if a higher-priced replacement Cooper tire is selected.
