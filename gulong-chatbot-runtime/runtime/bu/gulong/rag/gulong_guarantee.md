GULONG.PH 1-YEAR UNCONDITIONAL WARRANTY

Warranty Overview

The Gulong.ph Tire Protection Plan is an unconditional warranty on all types of tire damage, regardless of the cause. This warranty is applicable on selected brands purchased from the Gulong.ph website and official Facebook page, provided they are used under normal driving conditions. The warranty is valid only for tires installed by an authorized Installation Partner (IP) of Gulong.ph.

Warranty Period

This warranty is valid one (1) year from the date of tire installation.


Terms and Conditions for Gulong.ph Tire Protection Plan Claims


Eligibility

- The warranty is available exclusively to the original purchaser of the tire.
- Proof of purchase or warranty receipt is required for all warranty claims.
- The warranty period is counted from the purchase date on the original invoice, even if a tire is replaced under this warranty.

Tire Registration

To be eligible for coverage, customers must register their tire within thirty (30) days after installation using the Gulong.ph Warranty Registration Form provided by the Gulong.ph.


Inspection

Customers are required to bring the damaged tire to the Gulong.ph Main Office at 1166 Pasong Tamo Cor. Estrella St., Barangay San Antonio, Makati City for a technical assessment and inspection.


Replacement Process

- In-Stock Tires: If the tire is in stock, it will be replaced with a new tire of the same brand, pattern, and size as indicated in the original invoice.
- Out-of-Stock or Phased-Out Tires: If the tire is no longer available, Gulong.ph reserves the right to replace it with a comparable tire. If the price of the comparable tire is higher than the original tire, the customer is responsible for paying the price difference, based on the current selling price.


Exclusions

Tires that have been installed by service providers that are not affiliated with Gulong.ph's authorized Installation Partners.


Limitations

- Gulong.ph reserves the right to inspect the tire and confirm the damages before issuing a replacement.
- This warranty covers the replacement of the tire only and does not include any additional costs such as, but not limited to, mounting, balancing, alignment, or transportation.
- Gulong.ph will not be liable for any direct, incidental, or consequential damages arising from the use or inability to use the tire.
- The warranty claim is limited to changing the damaged tire to a new equivalent tire only and cannot be converted or compensated with cash or any discount.
- The warranty allows one replacement per tire purchase.
- All tires covered under this warranty are pro-rated based on their remaining tread depth.

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ 1: What is Tire Protection Plan?

The Gulong.ph Tire Protection Plan is our exclusive warranty program that provides coverage for your tires against all types of damage for one (1) year from the purchase date. This guarantee ensures that customers receive a free replacement for any damaged tire.

FAQ 2: What is Manufacturer's Warranty?

A manufacturer's warranty is a guarantee provided by the tire brand to cover defects that may arise due to issues in materials or production. This is different from extended, unconditional warranties or road hazard protection plans, which may cover accidental damage. Each tire manufacturer sets its own terms for the warranty, but Gulong.ph ensures that all products sold comply with their respective policies.

FAQ 3: What is the coverage of unconditional warranty?

Gulong.ph offers an unconditional tire warranty, covering all types of damage for one (1) year from the date of installation. As long as the customer registers their tire and presents it for inspection, a replacement tire will be provided - no questions asked.

FAQ 4: How do I register my Tire Protection Plan?

After your tire installation, you will receive an email with instructions on how to register your tires for the Gulong.ph Tire Protection Plan. Follow these steps:
- Open your email - check your inbox for the registration details.
- Fill out the registration form - complete the form and fill out the required details.
- Receive confirmation - once verified, you will receive a confirmation email that your tires are now covered under the Gulong.ph Tire Protection Plan.

Reminder: Register your tire within thirty (30) days after installation to be eligible for the Gulong.ph Tire Protection Plan.
Note: Only installed and on selected tire brands are covered by the Gulong.ph Tire Protection Plan. Please check the eligibility of your tire brand before registration.

FAQ 5: How do I track my warranty?

You can track your warranty by going to: Warranty Tracker (https://gulong.ph/warranty/track). Just input your email address and Order ID.

FAQ 6: How can I claim my Gulong.ph Tire Protection Plan?

If your tire gets damaged and you need to file a claim, follow these steps:
- Complete warranty claim form - fill out the warranty claim form by accessing it through GG Claim link (https://gulong.ph/warranty/claim).
- Verification process - our team will review your claim and will get in touch with you within 15 business days.
- Bring the damaged tire - visit the Gulong.ph Main Office for technical assessment and inspection.
- Get a replacement - once the damaged tire is inspected, you will receive a new equivalent tire.

FAQ 7: Do I need to bring my tire to Gulong.ph for a warranty claim?

Yes, you must bring your damaged tire to Gulong.ph's Head Office for a technical inspection and assessment. This step is required to validate your warranty claim and ensure a proper replacement.

FAQ 8: How long does my warranty claim process take?

Once your tire is inspected at Gulong.ph's Head Office, the warranty claim is processed on the same day in most cases. If stock availability affects the replacement, our team will inform you of the estimated fulfillment time.

FAQ 9: What is the difference between Gulong.ph Tire Protection Plan and Manufacturer's Warranty?

While the Manufacturer's Warranty covers defects due to production issues, the Gulong.ph Tire Protection Plan offers broader protection, including accidental damages.
