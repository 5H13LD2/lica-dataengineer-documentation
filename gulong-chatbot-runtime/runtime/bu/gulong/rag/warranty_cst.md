Brand: CST | Section: Overview
The CST Confidence Warranty Policy applies to CST brand radial passenger and ultra-light truck tires sold by AP Blue Whale Corp., North Trend Marketing Corp., and Solid Trend Trade Sales Inc. All CST tires covered by this policy are warranted to be free from manufacturing defects. Coverage applies for seven (7) years from the date of purchase or until the tread life of the tire is exhausted, whichever comes first.

Brand: CST | Section: Eligibility
The CST Confidence Warranty applies only if all of the following conditions are met:

* You are the original purchaser of a CST brand radial passenger or ultra-light truck tire
* The tire was purchased from an authorized dealer or distributor of AP Blue Whale Corp. or Solid Trend Trade Sales Inc.
* The tire has been used only on the vehicle on which it was originally installed
* The tire size and load range are appropriate for the vehicle based on the vehicle manufacturer’s recommendation
* A warranty claim is supported by the original proof of purchase presented to a CST authorized dealer or distributor

Brand: CST | Section: Coverage – Materials and Workmanship (First 10 Percent Wear)
If a CST radial passenger or ultra-light truck tire is determined unserviceable by an authorized dealer or distributor due to a covered condition not listed under exclusions, the tire will be replaced with the same or a comparable CST tire at no charge during the first ten percent (10%) of tread wear. Labor costs and taxes are not included.

Brand: CST | Section: Coverage – Pro-Rated Replacement (After 10 Percent Wear)
After the first ten percent (10%) of tread wear, if a CST radial passenger or light truck tire is determined unserviceable due to a covered condition, a pro-rated credit will be issued toward the purchase of a comparable new CST tire. The credit equals the percentage of usable tread remaining.

Brand: CST | Section: Usable Tread Definition
For CST warranty purposes, usable tread does not include the final 1.6 millimeters of tread depth. Tires worn beyond 1.6 millimeters are considered unsafe under Philippine law and are not eligible for warranty coverage.

Brand: CST | Section: Customer Responsibilities – Costs
Under the CST Confidence Warranty, the customer is responsible for all labor costs related to mounting and balancing the tire, as well as all applicable taxes.

Brand: CST | Section: Customer Responsibilities – Maintenance
To keep the CST Confidence Warranty valid, tires must be properly installed, air pressure checked regularly, and tires rotated, balanced, and alignment checked every 5,000 to 7,500 kilometers. All required maintenance costs are the responsibility of the customer.

Brand: CST | Section: Customer Responsibilities – Service Records
Service records for all required tire maintenance must be kept and presented when making a claim under the CST Confidence Warranty.

Brand: CST | Section: Important Notice
The CST Confidence Warranty does not apply to certain types of tire damage or wear. Customers should review the exclusions section carefully before filing a claim.

Brand: CST | Section: Exclusions – Road Hazards
The CST Confidence Warranty does not cover road hazard damage including cuts, snags, punctures, bruises, impact breaks, tire plugs, or any other damage caused by tire repair.

Brand: CST | Section: Exclusions – Improper Use and Maintenance
The CST Confidence Warranty does not cover tire failure or damage caused by improper operation or maintenance, including overloading, excessive speed, improper inflation practices that cause excessive operating temperatures, misapplication, vandalism, use of puncture sealants or fillers that affect pneumatic qualities, fire, or chemical corrosion.

Brand: CST | Section: Exclusions – Irregular Wear and Mechanical Issues
The CST Confidence Warranty does not cover irregular tread wear caused by improper wheel alignment, underinflation or overinflation, tire abuse such as spinning, improper mounting or dismounting, vehicle mechanical problems including faulty or worn brakes or suspension, damaged wheels, tire truing, snow or ice chain usage, flat spots from panic braking, accidents, or racing use.

Brand: CST | Section: Exclusions – Tire Alterations
The CST Confidence Warranty does not cover tires that have been altered, including decorative bead or sidewall materials or the addition of white inlay or whitewall features.

Brand: CST | Section: Exclusions – Ride and Vibration After Wear Threshold
The CST Confidence Warranty does not cover ride disturbance, vibration, or out-of-round conditions occurring after the first 1.6 millimeters of tread wear when not caused by a covered materials or workmanship defect.

Brand: CST | Section: Exclusions – Online Purchases and Consequential Loss
The CST Confidence Warranty does not cover tires purchased online unless purchased directly from the CST Authorized Online or E-commerce store. The warranty also does not cover loss of time, inconvenience, loss of use of the vehicle, or consequential damages.

Brand: CST | Section: Exclusions – Racing and Competition
The CST Confidence Warranty does not cover tires used for racing or other competition events.

Brand: CST | Section: Warranty Limitations
Any CST tire, regardless of construction quality, may fail or become unserviceable due to conditions beyond the manufacturer’s control. This warranty does not guarantee that tire failure will not occur.

Brand: CST | Section: Exclusive Remedy
Full or pro-rated replacement of a CST radial passenger tire is the sole and exclusive remedy available under this warranty.

Brand: CST | Section: Limitation of Liability
CST and its authorized dealers or distributors are not liable for incidental, indirect, special, or consequential damages, including loss of time, inconvenience, loss of vehicle use, towing, road service, or similar losses.

Brand: CST | Section: Scope of Coverage
This CST Confidence Warranty is limited to tire replacement only and does not cover property damage or physical injury caused by accidents or unforeseen events involving tire failure.

Brand: CST | Section: Tire Safety Guidance
CST tires are designed to deliver safety and performance, but proper use and maintenance are essential. Customers should always refer to the vehicle tire information placard, replace tires according to manufacturer-recommended specifications, and ensure tire speed ratings and load-carrying capacity are equal to or greater than the original equipment tires.
