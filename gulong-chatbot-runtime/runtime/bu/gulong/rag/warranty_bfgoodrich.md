Brand: BFGOODRICH | Section: Coverage – What Is Covered
BFGOODRICH warranty coverage depends on the time of sale relative to the tire’s manufacturing date.

For BFGOODRICH tires sold within three (3) years from the manufacturing date, the warranty applies for the life of the original usable tread pattern or six (6) years from the date of purchase, whichever comes first.

For BFGOODRICH tires sold three (3) years after the manufacturing date, the warranty applies for the life of the original usable tread pattern or six (6) years from the date of manufacture, whichever comes first.

At the end of the applicable period, all warranties, whether express or implied, are terminated.

Brand: BFGOODRICH | Section: Definition of Original Usable Tread
For BFGOODRICH warranty purposes, the original usable tread pattern refers to the tread depth down to the tread wear indicators, which is 1.6 millimeters of remaining tread depth.

Brand: BFGOODRICH | Section: Proof of Purchase and Date Basis
The date of purchase is determined by either:

* New vehicle registration documents when the tires are fitted on a new vehicle, or
* The original tire sales invoice

If no proof of purchase is available, the BFGOODRICH warranty period will be based on the date of manufacture.

Brand: BFGOODRICH | Section: Exclusions – What Is Not Covered
The BFGOODRICH warranty does not cover tires that become unserviceable due to any of the following circumstances:

* Road hazard injury such as cuts, snags, bruises, impact damage, or punctures, whether repairable or not
* Incorrect mounting, tire or wheel imbalance, or improper repair
* Under-inflation, over-inflation, improper maintenance, or other forms of abuse
* Mechanical irregularities in the vehicle, including wheel misalignment resulting in uneven or rapid wear
* Tires fitted to or used with incompatible or improper valves, rims, or wheels
* Vehicles carrying loads or operating at speeds exceeding the load and speed index marked on the tire sidewall or recommended for the vehicle
* Tires purchased second-hand
* Improper storage
* Tires not fitted or used in accordance with BFGOODRICH technical recommendations
* Accident, fire, chemical explosion, tire alteration, or vandalism
* Tires purchased from unauthorized dealers
* Climatic or ozone-related effects
