Brand: Dunlop | Section: Overview
The Dunlop Tires Warranty Policy applies to Dunlop brand tires sold in the Philippines. Dunlop tires come with an eight (8) year warranty counted from the production date and are designed to provide a safe and comfortable ride using Japanese technology.

Brand: Dunlop | Section: Eligibility
The Dunlop Tires Limited Warranty applies only if all of the following conditions are met:

* The tire is a Dunlop brand tire
* The tire was purchased from an Authorized Dunlop Dealer
* A valid invoice or official receipt can be presented for verification
* The tire is within eight (8) years from the production date

Brand: Dunlop | Section: Coverage – What Is Covered
The Dunlop warranty covers factory defects only.
Defective tires must be confirmed by Dunlop’s authorized technical team.
Approved warranty claims are compensated through a discount based on the remaining tread depth.
The discount is applied toward the purchase of a new Dunlop tire.

Brand: Dunlop | Section: Exclusions – What Is Not Covered
The Dunlop warranty does not cover damage caused by:

* Road hazards
* Customer mishandling
* Improper use or operation
* Any condition not classified as a factory defect

Brand: Dunlop | Section: Replacement and Compensation
Under the Dunlop warranty, no cash refunds are issued.
The value of the remaining tread depth is calculated by Dunlop’s technical team and is used to offset the cost of a new Dunlop tire.
Warranty compensation is provided only in the form of a discount and not as cash.

Brand: Dunlop | Section: Warranty Claim Requirements
To file a Dunlop warranty claim, the customer must:

* Present the tire for inspection
* Provide the invoice or official receipt
* Allow evaluation by the authorized Dunlop technical team
