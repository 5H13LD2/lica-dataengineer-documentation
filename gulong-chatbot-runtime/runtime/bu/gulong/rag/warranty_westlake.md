Brand: Westlake | Section: Overview
The Westlake Standard Limited Warranty applies to Westlake brand tires and warrants that each tire is free from defects in materials and workmanship, subject to the terms, conditions, and limitations stated in this policy. This warranty is valid for one (1) year from the original date of purchase.

Brand: Westlake | Section: Warranty Remedy
If examination determines that a Westlake product has failed due to a defect in materials or workmanship, Westlake will provide a reasonable allowance toward the purchase of a new product at current prices or another adjustment at its discretion.
All adjustments are made on a pro-rated basis based on the amount of usage or service received and are calculated using the most recent purchase price. Proof of purchase is required.

Brand: Westlake | Section: General Warranty Limitations
Any product, regardless of construction quality, may fail or become unserviceable due to conditions beyond the manufacturer’s control.
This warranty does not represent that tire, tube, or wheel failure cannot occur.

Brand: Westlake | Section: Coverage Eligibility
This Standard Limited Warranty applies only to products sold in and used on vehicles registered and operated in the Philippines.
The warranty applies only to the original purchaser and is not transferable.
Costs for mounting, balancing, and other associated services are not covered.

Brand: Westlake | Section: Exclusions – Road Hazards
The Westlake warranty does not cover damage caused by road hazards, including cuts, punctures (repairable or not), snags, bruises, tears, impact breaks, or similar conditions.

Brand: Westlake | Section: Exclusions – Misuse and Improper Use
The warranty does not cover products damaged due to misuse, including improper installation or mounting, misapplication, improper repair, overloading, improper inflation, excessive speed, racing, spinning, drifting, unintended off-road use, stone drilling, improper chain use, or use of non-approved or non-standard rims as determined by the Tire and Rim Association.

Brand: Westlake | Section: Exclusions – Mechanical and External Causes
The warranty does not cover damage caused by continued operation while severely under-inflated, wheel or rim misalignment or imbalance, defective brakes or shock absorbers, abuse, accident or collision, willful damage, oil or chemical action, improper storage, fire or excessive heat, vandalism, or water or other materials trapped inside the product.

Brand: Westlake | Section: Tire Adjustment Limits
Tire adjustments are based on remaining tread depth.
Tires worn to 2/32 inch tread depth or to the tread wear indicator bars are considered to have reached full useful life and are not adjustable for any reason.

Brand: Westlake | Section: Ride Disturbance and Out-of-Round Conditions
Tires returned for out-of-round or ride disturbance conditions within the first 2/32 inch of tread wear will be credited at 100 percent of value based on the average purchase price over the previous six months.
Tires returned for out-of-round conditions after the first 2/32 inch of wear are not eligible for adjustment.
Off-road, agriculture, and trailer tires are not eligible for out-of-round or ride disturbance consideration.

Brand: Westlake | Section: Special Tire Markings and Age Limits
Tires branded with special classifications such as “B” (blemished) may be excluded from adjustment at Westlake’s sole discretion.
Tires marked “NA” (non-adjustable) are excluded from this warranty.
Tires without a DOT number, tires modified by notching, buffing, or cutting, tires affected by aftermarket materials such as tire fill, sealant, or balancers, tires returned for weather or ozone cracking after six (6) months from purchase, and tires with production dates five (5) years or older are not covered.

Brand: Westlake | Section: Retreaded and Altered Tires
Tires that have been retreaded, except those covered under a separate Limited Casing Warranty, or altered in any way are not warranted.

Brand: Westlake | Section: Tube Warranty Limitations
Tube adjustments are considered on a case-by-case basis and must be claimed within thirty (30) days after installation or one (1) year from the date of purchase, whichever occurs first.

Brand: Westlake | Section: How to Obtain Warranty Service
To qualify for warranty credit, the dealer or customer must:

* Contact the Westlake Consumer Relations Department to request a Warranty Claim Form at [sales1@westlake.com.ph](mailto:sales1@westlake.com.ph)
* Use the official Westlake Warranty Claim Form and complete all required details, including tire size, tread pattern, DOT, and remaining tread
* Provide proof of original purchase showing vehicle mileage at the time of installation for mileage-based proration
* Submit the completed Claim Form and all required documents with the tire(s) to the Adjustment Center upon request

Brand: Westlake | Section: Inspection and Claim Resolution
Upon receipt, Westlake representatives will inspect the tire(s).
If adjustable, the dealer or customer will be notified and issued a credit, after which the tire will be destroyed by Westlake.
If not adjustable, the dealer or customer will receive written notification explaining the decision.
Non-adjustable tires requested for return will be shipped back at the dealer’s or customer’s expense. Tires left with Westlake will be destroyed without further notice.

Brand: Westlake | Section: Claim Processing Conditions
No credit will be issued until a completed Warranty Claim Form and all supporting documents are received and the tire is confirmed to be adjustable.
Illegible or incomplete forms may be returned for correction and will delay processing.

Brand: Westlake | Section: Returned Products Notice
All tires or wheels returned for warranty consideration are subject to inspection.
Items found not eligible for warranty adjustment will be disposed of unless prior return arrangements were made.
Any returned items are shipped back at the dealer’s or customer’s expense.

Brand: Westlake | Section: Contact Information
Questions, information requests, and warranty claims must be submitted via email to [sales1@westlake.com.ph](mailto:sales1@westlake.com.ph).
Claims not submitted according to Westlake’s standard claim procedures or without prior authorization will not be processed and may be subject to freight chargeback.
