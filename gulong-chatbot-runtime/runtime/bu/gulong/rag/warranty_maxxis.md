Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Overview
MAXXIS Confidence Warranty Policy: All MAXXIS tires sold by AP Blue Whale Corp., North Trend Marketing Corp., and Solid Trend Trade Sales Inc. are free from manufacturing defects. This MAXXIS Confidence Warranty covers seven (7) years from the date of purchase or until the tread life of the tire is exhausted, whichever comes first.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Eligibility
The MAXXIS Confidence Warranty applies only if all of the following conditions are met:

* You are the original purchaser of a MAXXIS brand radial passenger or ultra-light truck tire
* The tire was purchased from an authorized dealer or distributor of AP Blue Whale Corp. or Solid Trend Trade Sales Inc.
* The tire has been used only on the vehicle on which it was originally installed
* The tire size and load range match the vehicle manufacturer’s recommendation
* A warranty claim is supported by the original proof of purchase presented to a MAXXIS authorized dealer or distributor

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Coverage – Materials and Workmanship (First 10 Percent Wear)
If a MAXXIS radial passenger or ultra-light truck tire is determined unserviceable by an authorized dealer or distributor due to a covered condition (not listed under exclusions), the tire will be replaced with the same or a comparable MAXXIS tire at no charge during the first ten percent (10%) of tread wear. Labor costs and taxes are not included.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Coverage – Pro-Rated Replacement (After 10 Percent Wear)
After the first ten percent (10%) of tread wear, if a MAXXIS radial passenger or light truck tire is determined unserviceable due to a covered condition, a pro-rated credit will be issued. The credit equals the percentage of usable tread remaining and is applied toward the purchase of a comparable new MAXXIS tire.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Usable Tread Definition
For MAXXIS warranty purposes, usable tread does not include the final 1.6 millimeters of tread depth. Tires worn beyond 1.6 millimeters are considered unsafe under Philippine law and are not eligible for warranty coverage.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Customer Responsibilities – Costs
Under the MAXXIS Confidence Warranty, the customer is responsible for all labor costs, mounting and balancing charges, and applicable taxes related to tire replacement.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Customer Responsibilities – Maintenance
To keep the MAXXIS Confidence Warranty valid, tires must be properly installed, air pressure must be checked regularly, and tires must be rotated, balanced, and alignment checked every 5,000 to 7,500 kilometers. All maintenance costs are the customer’s responsibility.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Customer Responsibilities – Service Records
Service records for all required tire maintenance must be kept and presented when making a claim under the MAXXIS Confidence Warranty.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Important Notice
The MAXXIS Confidence Warranty does not apply to certain types of tire damage or wear. Customers are advised to carefully review the exclusions section before filing a claim.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Exclusions – Road Hazards
The MAXXIS Confidence Warranty does not cover road hazard damage including cuts, snags, punctures, bruises, impact breaks, tire plugs, or any damage caused by tire repair.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Exclusions – Improper Use and Maintenance
The MAXXIS Confidence Warranty does not cover tire failure or damage resulting from overloading, excessive speed, improper inflation, excessive operating temperatures beyond specifications, misapplication, vandalism, use of puncture sealants or fillers affecting pneumatic qualities, fire, or chemical corrosion.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Exclusions – Irregular Wear and Mechanical Issues
The MAXXIS Confidence Warranty does not cover irregular tread wear caused by improper wheel alignment, underinflation or overinflation, tire abuse such as spinning, improper mounting or dismounting, vehicle mechanical problems (including brakes or suspension), damaged wheels, tire truing, snow or ice chain usage, flat spots from panic braking, accidents, or racing use.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Exclusions – Tire Alterations
The MAXXIS Confidence Warranty does not cover tires that have been altered, including decorative bead or sidewall materials or adding white inlay (whitewall) features.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Exclusions – Ride and Vibration After Wear Threshold
The MAXXIS Confidence Warranty does not cover ride disturbance, vibration, or out-of-round conditions occurring after the first 1.6 millimeters of tread wear when not caused by a covered materials or workmanship defect.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Exclusions – Online Purchases and Consequential Loss
The MAXXIS Confidence Warranty does not cover tires purchased online unless purchased directly from the MAXXIS Authorized Online or E-commerce Store. The warranty also does not cover loss of time, inconvenience, loss of vehicle use, or consequential damages.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Exclusions – Racing and Competition
The MAXXIS Confidence Warranty does not cover tires used for racing or competition events.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Warranty Limitations
Any MAXXIS tire, regardless of construction quality, may fail or become unserviceable due to conditions beyond the manufacturer’s control. This warranty does not guarantee that tire failure will not occur.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Exclusive Remedy
Replacement of the MAXXIS tire, whether full or pro-rated, is the sole and exclusive remedy available under this warranty.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Limitation of Liability
MAXXIS and its authorized dealers or distributors are not liable for incidental, indirect, special, or consequential damages including loss of time, inconvenience, loss of vehicle use, towing, road service, or similar losses.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Scope of Coverage
This MAXXIS Confidence Warranty is limited to tire replacement only and does not cover property damage or physical injury caused by accidents or unforeseen events involving tire failure.

Brand: MAXXIS | Section: Tire Safety Guidance
MAXXIS tires are designed for safety and performance. Customers should always refer to the vehicle tire information placard and replace tires according to manufacturer-recommended specifications. Tire speed ratings and load capacity must be equal to or greater than the original equipment tires.
