Brand: Vredestein | Section: Overview
This warranty applies to Vredestein tires sold in the Philippines and provides coverage for defects in workmanship and materials, subject to the terms and conditions stated in this policy.

Brand: Vredestein | Section: Warranty Term
The Vredestein warranty applies for the life of the original usable tread or for six (6) years from the date of purchase, whichever occurs first.
Coverage includes a limited warranty against defects in workmanship and materials for the same period.

Brand: Vredestein | Section: Definition of Original Usable Tread
For Vredestein warranty purposes, the life of the original usable tread refers to the original tread depth down to the level of the tread wear indicators.

Brand: Vredestein | Section: Exclusions – Road Hazards and Damage
The Vredestein warranty does not cover tires that become unserviceable due to road hazard injuries, including cuts, snags, bruises, impact damage, or punctures.

Brand: Vredestein | Section: Exclusions – Incorrect Fitting and Misapplication
The Vredestein warranty does not cover tires damaged due to incorrect fitting, including mounting the tire on the wrong wheel rim size or using improper mounting or dismounting equipment or tools.
Misapplication, such as using the tire in unsuitable road conditions, is also excluded.

Brand: Vredestein | Section: Exclusions – Improper Maintenance and Overloading
The Vredestein warranty does not cover damage caused by improper maintenance, including under-inflation, over-inflation, abuse resulting in casing damage or fatigue, or overloading.

Brand: Vredestein | Section: Exclusions – Accidents and External Causes
The Vredestein warranty does not cover damage caused by accidents, fire, chemical corrosion, contamination, tire alteration, or vandalism.

Brand: Vredestein | Section: Exclusions – Storage and Added Materials
The Vredestein warranty does not cover flat spotting caused by improper storage.
Damage caused by the addition of liquid, solid, or gaseous materials other than air or nitrogen is excluded.

Brand: Vredestein | Section: Exclusions – Load, Speed, and Mechanical Irregularities
The Vredestein warranty does not cover tires used at loads or speeds greater than the ISO-recommended load or speed values.
Uneven or rapid wear caused by vehicle mechanical irregularities, such as axle misalignment that damages the under-tread, carcass, or steel belts, is not covered.

Brand: Vredestein | Section: Exclusions – Ozone and Weather Effects
The Vredestein warranty does not cover ozone or weather-related cracking.

Brand: Vredestein | Section: Conditions – Damages Not Covered
This Vredestein warranty does not provide compensation for loss of time, loss of use of the vehicle, inconvenience, or incidental or consequential damages.

Brand: Vredestein | Section: Tire Custody and Ownership
Tires presented for warranty claim remain the property of the owner or consumer.
The retailer accepts no responsibility for loss of or damage to tires while they are in the retailer’s custody or control for inspection.
Tires accepted for warranty claim become the property of RYCO Inc.
In the event of a dispute, the owner or consumer must make the tire available for further inspection.

Brand: Vredestein | Section: Territorial Limitation
This Vredestein warranty applies only within the Republic of the Philippines.
