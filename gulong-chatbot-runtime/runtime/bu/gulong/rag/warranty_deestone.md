Brand: Deestone | Section: Warranty Overview
The Deestone tire warranty applies to Deestone brand tires and remains valid as long as the tire has at least 2/32 inch of original tread remaining in all positions, or up to four (4) years from the production date, whichever comes first.

Brand: Deestone | Section: Tread Wear Limitation
Deestone tires with less than 2/32 inch of original tread remaining are considered to have delivered their original life and are not eligible for warranty coverage or adjustment.

Brand: Deestone | Section: Exclusions – Road Hazards and Accidents
The Deestone warranty does not cover damage caused by road hazards, including punctures, cuts, snags, or impact breaks.
Damage resulting from wrecks, collisions, or fire is also excluded.

Brand: Deestone | Section: Exclusions – Inflation, Load, and Mounting
The Deestone warranty does not cover damage caused by improper inflation, overloading, or improper mounting or demounting of the tire.

Brand: Deestone | Section: Exclusions – Improper Operation or Maintenance
The Deestone warranty does not cover damage resulting from improper operation or maintenance, including conditions that cause excessive operating temperatures beyond the tire’s designed capabilities.
