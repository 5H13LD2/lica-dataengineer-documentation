Brand: Apollo | Section: Coverage – Warranty Overview
Gulong.ph offers an unconditional warranty covering all types of tire damage, regardless of cause. This warranty applies to Apollo Tires purchased through the Gulong.ph website or official Facebook page and used under normal driving conditions. Coverage is valid only for Apollo tires installed by authorized Gulong.ph Installation Partners.

Brand: Apollo | Section: Warranty Period
The Apollo tire warranty provided by Gulong.ph is valid for one (1) year from the date of purchase.

Brand: Apollo | Section: Eligibility
The Apollo warranty is available exclusively to the original purchaser of the tire.
Proof of purchase or a valid warranty receipt is required for all warranty claims.
The warranty period is counted from the purchase date shown on the original invoice, even if an Apollo tire is replaced under this warranty.

Brand: Apollo | Section: Tire Subscription Requirement
To be eligible for Apollo warranty coverage under Gulong.ph, customers must register their tire within thirty (30) days of purchase using the Gulong.ph Warranty Subscription Form.
The following information must be provided during registration:

* Purchase date
* Installation date
* Tire brand, pattern, size, and DOT
* Order ID
* Customer contact information

Brand: Apollo | Section: Inspection Requirement
Customers must bring the damaged Apollo tire to the Gulong.ph Main Office for technical assessment and inspection.
Inspection location: 1166 Pasong Tamo corner Estrella Street, Barangay San Antonio, Makati City.

Brand: Apollo | Section: Replacement Process – In-Stock Tires
If the damaged Apollo tire is in stock, Gulong.ph will replace it with a new Apollo tire of the same brand, pattern, and size as stated on the original invoice.

Brand: Apollo | Section: Replacement Process – Out-of-Stock or Phased-Out Tires
If the original Apollo tire is no longer available, Gulong.ph may replace it with a comparable tire.
If the comparable tire has a higher current selling price, the customer is responsible for paying the price difference.

Brand: Apollo | Section: Exclusions – Installation Partner Requirement
The Apollo warranty does not cover tires installed by service providers that are not authorized Gulong.ph Installation Partners.

Brand: Apollo | Section: Claim Process – Filing a Claim
To initiate an Apollo warranty claim, the customer must:

* Notify Gulong.ph via email or phone regarding the issue
* Complete the Gulong.ph Warranty Claim Form

Brand: Apollo | Section: Claim Process – Verification and Inspection
The customer must present the damaged Apollo tire and proof of purchase and/or warranty receipt to an authorized Gulong.ph representative for verification and inspection.

Brand: Apollo | Section: Claim Process – Replacement
If damage is confirmed after inspection, Gulong.ph will replace the Apollo tire with a new tire of the same brand or a comparable model, subject to availability and policy terms.

Brand: Apollo | Section: Limitations – Inspection and Approval
Gulong.ph reserves the right to inspect all Apollo tires and confirm the extent of damage before approving any replacement.

Brand: Apollo | Section: Limitations – Scope of Coverage
The Apollo warranty covers replacement of the tire only.
Additional costs such as mounting, balancing, alignment, transportation, or related services are not included.

Brand: Apollo | Section: Limitations – Liability
Gulong.ph is not liable for any direct, incidental, or consequential damages arising from the use of, or inability to use, the Apollo tire.

Brand: Apollo | Section: Limitations – Compensation Rules
Apollo warranty claims are limited to tire replacement only and cannot be converted to cash, discounts, or other forms of compensation.
The warranty allows one (1) replacement per tire purchase.

Brand: Apollo | Section: Proration Policy
All Apollo tires covered under this warranty are subject to proration based on remaining tread depth.

Brand: Apollo | Section: Commitment Statement
Gulong.ph is committed to delivering high-quality Apollo tires and ensuring complete customer satisfaction. Customers can rely on Gulong.ph for quality service and full support in the event of Apollo tire damage.
