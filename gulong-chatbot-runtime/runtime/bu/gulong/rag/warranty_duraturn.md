Brand: Duraturn | Section: Overview
The Duraturn Consumer Tires Limited Manufacturer’s Warranty applies to new Duraturn tires sold in the Philippines. Duraturn warrants that every new Duraturn tire is free from defects in workmanship or materials for five (5) years from the date of manufacture (DOT code) or for the life of the original usable tread, whichever comes first.

Brand: Duraturn | Section: Warranty Term and Tread Definition
The warranty covers the life of the original usable tread, which ends when the tire tread has worn down to 1.6 millimeters of remaining tread depth.
A tire is considered fully worn out once only 1.6 millimeters of tread depth remains and is no longer eligible for warranty coverage beyond this point.

Brand: Duraturn | Section: Warranty Obligation – Free Replacement
If a Duraturn tire is found to have failed due to a defect in workmanship or material during the first 1.6 millimeters of the original tread, Duraturn will replace the tire with a comparable new Duraturn tire free of charge.
The owner is responsible for applicable taxes, mounting, and balancing costs.

Brand: Duraturn | Section: Warranty Obligation – Pro-Rated Credit
If a Duraturn tire fails due to a defect after the first 1.6 millimeters of the original usable tread has worn, a pro-rated credit will be issued.
The credit is calculated based on the proportion of the original usable tread remaining on the defective tire relative to the original usable tread of a new tire.
The owner is responsible for applicable taxes, mounting, and balancing costs.

Brand: Duraturn | Section: Adjustment for Balance or Roundness
Adjustment for out-of-balance or out-of-round conditions is allowed only during the first 1.6 millimeters of the original tread.

Brand: Duraturn | Section: Eligibility and Ownership Limitations
All Duraturn warranties apply only to the original purchaser and the original vehicle on which the tire was mounted.
The warranty is not transferable to subsequent owners or vehicles.

Brand: Duraturn | Section: Territorial Limitation
This Duraturn limited warranty applies only within the Philippines.
Tires used on vehicles registered or operated outside the Philippines are not covered.

Brand: Duraturn | Section: Exclusions – Non-Adjustable and Prior Adjustments
The Duraturn warranty does not cover tires branded or marked as “Non-Adjustable” (N/A) or tires that have been previously adjusted.

Brand: Duraturn | Section: Exclusions – Damage and Irregular Wear
The Duraturn warranty does not cover failure, damage, or irregular wear caused by:

* Road hazards such as punctures, cuts, snags, impact breaks, wrecks, or collisions
* Fire, accidents, or vandalism
* Vehicle mechanical conditions including misalignment, wheel imbalance, faulty shocks, or brakes
* Misapplication of the tire or use of improper inner tubes
* Alteration of the tire, addition of foreign materials, or transfer of the tire from one vehicle to another

Brand: Duraturn | Section: Exclusions – Costs and Consequential Damages
The Duraturn warranty does not cover:

* Mounting and balancing service costs
* Costs related to tire pressure monitoring systems
* Loss of time, loss of use, inconvenience, or incidental or consequential damages

Brand: Duraturn | Section: Owner Obligations – Claim Requirements
To be eligible for Duraturn warranty service, the owner must:

* Present the defective tire to the authorized Duraturn dealer where the tire was originally purchased
* Present proof of purchase acceptable to the dealer
* Complete and sign a Duraturn Warranty Claim Form available at authorized dealers
* Pay any amount due on the replacement tire, including taxes, mounting, balancing, and other services ordered, less the applicable warranty credit

This Duraturn limited warranty is exclusive and replaces any other expressed or implied warranties regarding the quality of Duraturn tires.
All remedies for breach of warranty are limited strictly to those specifically provided under this policy.
