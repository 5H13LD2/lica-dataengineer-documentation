Brand: Bridgestone / Firestone | Section: Overview
This warranty policy applies to eligible passenger and light truck tires under the Bridgestone and Firestone brands. The Limited Warranty applies only to the original purchaser and is subject to the eligibility rules, coverage limits, and exclusions stated below.

Brand: Bridgestone / Firestone | Section: Eligibility
The Bridgestone / Firestone Limited Warranty is valid only if all of the following conditions are met:

* The tire was purchased from an official Bridgestone retailer
* Authorized retailers are listed at [https://bridgestonetires.com.ph/tire-center](https://bridgestonetires.com.ph/tire-center)
* The tire size and speed rating are equal to or higher than the vehicle manufacturer’s specification
* The tire has been used only on the vehicle where it was originally installed
* The tire has not been used in commercial service
* The tire is not imported or manufactured from Costa Rica, South America, or South Africa


Brand: Bridgestone / Firestone | Section: Coverage – What Is Covered
The Bridgestone / Firestone Limited Warranty covers an eligible tire that becomes unusable due to manufacturing defects or any condition within the manufacturer’s control.
Covered tires will be replaced with an equivalent new tire.
Coverage terms depend on the specific tire brand and are subject to the warranty coverage rules described below.


Brand: Bridgestone / Firestone | Section: Exclusions – Road Hazard Damage
The Bridgestone / Firestone Limited Warranty does not cover road hazard damage, including punctures, cuts, impact breaks, stone drilling, bruises, bulges, or snags.



Brand: Bridgestone / Firestone | Section: Exclusions – Improper Use or Operation
The warranty does not cover damage caused by improper use or operation, including improper inflation pressure, overloading, tire or wheel spinning, use of an improper wheel, tire chain damage, misuse, misapplication, negligence, tire alteration, or racing and competition use.


Brand: Bridgestone / Firestone | Section: Exclusions – Improper or Insufficient Maintenance
The warranty does not cover damage or irregular wear caused by insufficient or improper maintenance, including failure to rotate tires as recommended by the vehicle manufacturer, wheel misalignment, worn suspension components, improper mounting or dismounting, tire or wheel imbalance, or other vehicle-related defects or conditions.


Brand: Bridgestone / Firestone | Section: Exclusions – Contamination or External Damage
The warranty does not cover damage caused by petroleum products or other chemicals, fire or externally generated heat, or water or foreign material trapped inside the tire during mounting or inflation.


Brand: Bridgestone / Firestone | Section: Exclusions – Other Conditions
The Bridgestone / Firestone Limited Warranty does not cover:

* Tires that have been repaired, as any repair voids the warranty
* Run-Flat (RFT) tires used improperly under low or no tire pressure, including exceeding speed or distance limitations
* Rapid tread wear or normal wear-out
* Ride disturbance or vibration after 1/32 inch (0.8 mm) of tread wear
* Tires with sealant, balancing, or filler materials not applied by the manufacturer
* Tires used in commercial service
* Tires purchased as used
* Tires inflated with substances other than air or nitrogen


Brand: Bridgestone / Firestone | Section: Covered Tire Types
The warranty covers all Bridgestone brand passenger and light truck tires.
Temporary-use spare tires are excluded from coverage.


Brand: Bridgestone / Firestone | Section: Replacement Terms
A covered Bridgestone or Firestone tire will be replaced with an equivalent new tire either free of charge during the free replacement period or on a pro-rated basis after the free replacement period.


Brand: Bridgestone / Firestone | Section: Coverage Duration
Coverage duration is measured from the date of manufacture:

* Bridgestone tires are covered for up to seven (7) years
* Firestone tires are covered for up to five (5) years

During this period, replacement cost is calculated as the percentage of tread wear multiplied by the dealer’s current selling price.
Additional charges may apply, including taxes, mounting, balancing, disposal fees, and other service charges.



Brand: Bridgestone / Firestone | Section: Tread Wear Requirement
To qualify for coverage, the tire must have more than 2/32 inch (1.6 mm) of remaining original tread depth.
Tires worn down to the tread wear indicators are not covered.



Brand: Bridgestone / Firestone | Section: Original Equipment Tires
Original equipment tires supplied on new vehicles are covered under a separate vehicle manufacturer warranty.
Customers should refer to the warranty documentation found in the vehicle glove box.



Brand: Bridgestone / Firestone | Section: Replacement Tire Warranty
Replacement tires provided under the Bridgestone / Firestone Limited Warranty are covered by the manufacturer’s warranty applicable at the time of replacement.
Coverage terms for replacement tires may differ from the original tire.



Brand: Bridgestone / Firestone | Section: Owner Responsibilities
To keep the Limited Warranty valid, the owner must:

* Regularly inspect tires
* Maintain proper inflation pressure as specified by the vehicle manufacturer
* Operate within tire load capacity and speed limitations
* Maintain proper wheel alignment
* Maintain proper tire and wheel assembly balance



Brand: Bridgestone / Firestone | Section: Warranty Claim Process
To request a warranty adjustment, the tire must be presented to an authorized Bridgestone retailer for inspection.
