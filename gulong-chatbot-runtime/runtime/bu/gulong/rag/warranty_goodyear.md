Brand: Goodyear | Section: Overview
The Goodyear 5-Year Limited Warranty applies to eligible passenger and SUV tires purchased in the Philippines. The warranty is valid for five (5) years from the date of purchase and covers eligible Goodyear tires subject to the terms, conditions, and exclusions stated in this policy. This warranty applies to tires purchased on or after January 1, 2017.

Brand: Goodyear | Section: Warranty Period Limitation
The Goodyear warranty does not apply to tires that are more than five (5) years from the date of purchase.

Brand: Goodyear | Section: Tread Depth Limitation
The Goodyear warranty does not cover tires with actual remaining usable tread depth below twenty percent (20%) of the original usable tread depth.

Brand: Goodyear | Section: Exclusions – Tire Modifications
The Goodyear warranty does not cover tires modified by any material added after leaving a Goodyear factory.
No warranty credit will be given for tires modified with balancing substances, white inlay on black tires, sealants, or fillers, excluding Duraseal technology, if the condition relates to such materials.

Brand: Goodyear | Section: Exclusions – Wear and Maintenance
The Goodyear warranty does not cover wear-related complaints including fast wear, uneven wear, shoulder wear, diagonal wear, spot wear, or heel-and-toe wear caused by:

* Poor suspension condition
* Steering or braking system issues
* Vehicle misalignment
* Improper toe or camber adjustment
* Improper inflation
* Overloading
* High-speed spin-up

Brand: Goodyear | Section: Exclusions – Misuse and Racing
The Goodyear warranty does not cover tires that are misapplied, misused, neglected, or used for racing.
This includes tires used as temporary or spare tires on racing vehicles or passenger vehicles used for special applications such as police pursuit service.

Brand: Goodyear | Section: Exclusions – Road Hazards
The Goodyear warranty does not cover damage caused by road hazards including cuts, snags, impact breaks, punctures, scratches, abrasions, multiple cuts, chipping, flaking, chunking, or damage from wrecks, collisions, or fire.
This exclusion applies whether the tire is repairable or not.

Brand: Goodyear | Section: Exclusions – Ride Disturbance and Vibration
The Goodyear warranty does not cover ride disturbances or vibrations occurring after the first twenty-five percent (25%) of usable tread wear or caused by damaged wheels or any vehicle-related condition.

Brand: Goodyear | Section: Exclusions – Air Leakage
The Goodyear warranty does not cover air leakage on tires that have been mounted for more than three (3) months from the date of purchase.

Brand: Goodyear | Section: Exclusions – Long-Term Storage
The Goodyear warranty does not cover flat spots or sidewall deformation caused by long-term storage of vehicles with tires mounted, including spare tires.

Brand: Goodyear | Section: Exclusions – Repairs and Tampering
The Goodyear warranty does not cover tires with conditions resulting from structural modification or tampering, including improper third-party repairs, poor or improper mounting or demounting, siping, or use with defective rims or wheels.

Brand: Goodyear | Section: Exclusions – Tube Usage
The Goodyear warranty does not cover tubeless tires that are used with inner tubes.

Brand: Goodyear | Section: Exclusions – External Damage
The Goodyear warranty does not cover tires damaged by external events including mounting or dismounting damage, vehicle mechanical condition issues, or chain damage.

Brand: Goodyear | Section: Manufacturer Limitation
Any Goodyear tire, regardless of quality or construction, may develop conditions in service that make it unserviceable due to factors beyond the manufacturer’s control. This warranty does not represent that tire conditions will not develop.

Brand: Goodyear | Section: No Additional Warranty
There is no other guarantee, warranty, or liability, express or implied, applicable to Goodyear products beyond what is stated in this policy. No representative has authority to make additional representations, promises, or agreements.

Brand: Goodyear | Section: Territorial Limitation
The Goodyear 5-Year Limited Warranty applies only within the Philippines.

Brand: Goodyear | Section: Tire Custody and Ownership
Tires presented to an authorized Goodyear dealership for inspection remain the property of the consumer.
Goodyear assumes no responsibility for loss or damage to tires while in dealership custody.
Once a tire is adjusted and approved under warranty, ownership of the tire transfers to Goodyear.
In the event of a dispute, the consumer is responsible for taking the tire for further inspection.

Brand: Goodyear | Section: Costs and Damages Not Covered
The Goodyear warranty does not provide compensation for service charges, mounting and balancing costs, loss of time, loss of vehicle use, inconvenience, or consequential damages. Coverage is limited insofar as permitted by law.
