Brand: Toyo | Section: Overview
The Toyo Tires Warranty applies to Toyo brand tires sold in the Philippines. The warranty period is eight (8) years from the production date and covers factory defects only, subject to the terms and conditions stated in this policy.

Brand: Toyo | Section: Eligibility
The Toyo warranty applies only if all of the following conditions are met:

* The tire is a genuine Toyo tire
* The tire was purchased from an Authorized Toyo Tires Dealer
* A valid invoice or official receipt is presented for verification
* The receipt is from Tireshakk or other authorized and accredited dealers
* The tire was imported by Armstrong Enterprises Co., Inc. to ensure authenticity
* Legitimate Toyo tires sold in the Philippines are imported from Japan or Malaysia

Brand: Toyo | Section: Coverage – What Is Covered
The Toyo warranty covers factory defects only.
Defective tires must be evaluated and confirmed by the Toyo technical team to qualify for warranty coverage.
Approved claims are granted a discount corresponding to the remaining tire tread depth.

Brand: Toyo | Section: Exclusions – What Is Not Covered
The Toyo warranty does not cover damage caused by:

* Road hazards
* Customer mishandling
* Improper use or operation

Brand: Toyo | Section: Replacement and Compensation
Under the Toyo warranty, no cash refunds are issued.
The value corresponding to the remaining tread depth is used to offset the cost of a new Toyo tire.
Warranty compensation is provided only as a discount applied to the replacement tire purchase.

Brand: Toyo | Section: Claim Requirements
To file a Toyo warranty claim, the customer must present the tire together with the invoice or official receipt to an authorized Toyo Tires dealer for verification and technical evaluation.
