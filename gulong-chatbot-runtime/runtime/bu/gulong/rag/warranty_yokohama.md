Brand: Yokohama | Section: Coverage – What the Warranty Covers
The Yokohama Warranty covers products with manufacturing defects, subject to the terms and conditions of this policy.
The warranty also applies to Yokohama tires used under normal operating conditions with proper application and maintenance.
This warranty does not cover deterioration, damage, failure, impairment, changes in performance, or any condition that causes Yokohama tires to become unserviceable due to the absence of proper maintenance.

Brand: Yokohama | Section: Eligibility
This warranty applies to every genuine Yokohama replacement tire for passenger cars and light trucks bearing the Yokohama brand name.
The tire must be exclusively distributed by YTSPI and used under normal service conditions in the Philippines.
Normal service means proper tire application and proper maintenance.
All warranty claims are subject to evaluation and final approval by YTSPI’s certified warranty claim adjuster.

Brand: Yokohama | Section: Coverage Duration
Yokohama replacement tires for passenger cars and light trucks are warranted against unserviceable conditions for a period of sixty (60) months from the date of manufacture.
Unserviceable conditions or damage arising from excluded incidents or conditions are not covered.
For warranty purposes, “unserviceable” means unusable and non-repairable.

Brand: Yokohama | Section: Prorated Warranty Basis
Warranty claims are limited to sixty (60) months from the date of manufacture.
If a Yokohama tire becomes unserviceable for causes not excluded, it will be covered on a usable tread wear prorated basis.
Tread wear is calculated as a percentage of the groove depth between the original usable groove depth and the remaining usable tread groove.
The last 1.6 millimeters of groove depth is not included in the tread wear computation.

Brand: Yokohama | Section: Exclusions – Added Materials and Modifications
The Yokohama warranty does not cover tires that have had materials added after leaving the manufacturing plant, including fillers, sealants, balancing substances, or external tire treatments.
If added materials are the cause of failure or damage, the tire will not be accepted for warranty credit.

Brand: Yokohama | Section: Exclusions – Maintenance, Negligence, and Mechanical Conditions
The Yokohama warranty does not cover deterioration, damage, failure, or changes in performance caused by lack of proper maintenance, negligence, or fault of the buyer, owner, or customer.
This includes premature or irregular wear caused by defective vehicle mechanical conditions such as misalignment, wheel imbalance, or faulty shock absorbers or brakes.

Brand: Yokohama | Section: Exclusions – Road Hazards and External Events
The Yokohama warranty does not cover loss, damage, or failure caused by fortuitous events, acts of God, or external factors.
Road hazard damage is excluded, including cuts, punctures (repairable or not), bruises, tears, abrasions, or impact breaks.

Brand: Yokohama | Section: Exclusions – Tampering and Out-of-Round Conditions
The Yokohama warranty does not cover tires that have been tampered with, including alterations to serial numbers, ply ratings, tire size, or similar identifiers.
Out-of-round conditions occurring after the first twenty percent (20%) of usable groove depth are not covered.

Brand: Yokohama | Section: Exclusions – Environmental and Inflation-Related Damage
The Yokohama warranty does not cover damage caused by climatic or ozone effects, including long exposure to rain, sun, or chemicals.
Damage caused by improper inflation pressure or tires that have been run while flat is excluded.

Brand: Yokohama | Section: Exclusions – Wear Limits and Fitment Issues
The Yokohama warranty does not cover tires worn down to 1.6 millimeters or less of remaining groove depth.
The warranty does not cover tires fitted to or used with incompatible or improper valves, rims, or wheels.
Improper tire size or specification application is also excluded.

Brand: Yokohama | Section: Exclusions – Handling, Storage, and Other Damage
The Yokohama warranty does not cover damage caused by accidents, corrosion, vandalism, fire, or natural causes.
Flat spots caused while the tire is immobile are excluded.
Damage resulting from improper mounting or dismounting procedures or improper storage is not covered.

Brand: Yokohama | Section: Customer Obligations – Claim Requirements
To file a claim under the Yokohama warranty, the customer must present:

* The Yokohama tire subject of the claim
* Proof of purchase of the tire
* The Limited Warranty or Adjustment Program Certificate

Brand: Yokohama | Section: Customer Obligations – Ownership and Costs
Tires replaced under this warranty become the property of YTSPI and must be surrendered by the customer.
If the replacement tire price is higher than the original warranted tire price, the customer is responsible for paying the difference between the adjustment base price and the original purchase price.
The customer is also responsible for all allied service charges, including mounting, dismounting, rotation, alignment, applicable taxes, and related costs.
