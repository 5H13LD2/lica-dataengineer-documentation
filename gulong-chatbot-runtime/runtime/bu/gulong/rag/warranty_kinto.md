Brand: KINTO | Section: Warranty Overview
The KINTO tire warranty provides coverage for two (2) years or fifty thousand (50,000) kilometers, whichever comes first. This warranty applies only to the conditions specified in this policy. Any conflicting statements found in distributorship or other contracts are considered void.

Brand: KINTO | Section: Warranty Validity Conditions
The KINTO warranty ends when the tire tread depth reaches 1.6 millimeters, taking into consideration the initial level of damage.
Determination of warranty eligibility is at the discretion of the Company, and inspection of damaged tires must be carried out only by authorized representatives.
The Company and/or Distributor are not responsible for items removed from the vehicle or modified by another party after installation.
The Company reserves the right to amend or terminate this warranty policy without prior notice.

Brand: KINTO | Section: Warranty Exclusions – Accidents and Misuse
The KINTO warranty does not cover accidents, injuries, or damages resulting from crimes, intentional acts, fire, theft, car racing, misuse, or use for an incorrect purpose.

Brand: KINTO | Section: Exclusions – Improper Installation and Repairs
KINTO tires are not covered if they are removed or reinstalled improperly, have impaired balance, or are used with repaired tires or wheel drums.
Retreaded or repaired tires are not covered.

Brand: KINTO | Section: Exclusions – Inflation and Flat Tire Damage
The KINTO warranty does not cover damage caused by improper inflation pressure, running the tire while flat, or operating the vehicle with no air pressure or pressure below the specified standard.

Brand: KINTO | Section: Exclusions – Mechanical Conditions and Wear
The KINTO warranty does not cover premature or irregular wear caused by defective vehicle mechanical conditions, including incorrect wheel alignment, wheel imbalance, faulty brakes, or shock absorbers.
Tires worn down to 1.6 millimeters or less of remaining groove depth are considered worn out and are not covered.

Brand: KINTO | Section: Exclusions – Load, Speed, and Misapplication
The KINTO warranty does not cover tires worn or damaged due to overloading beyond prescribed standards or overspeeding beyond the limits specified in the manufacturer’s tire manual.
Misuse includes using passenger car tires on pickup trucks or vans.

Brand: KINTO | Section: Exclusions – Wheels, Valves, and Compatibility
The KINTO warranty does not cover tires installed or used with valves, wheel drums, wheel pans, or wheels that are improper or incompatible with the tire type or do not meet vehicle manual specifications.

Brand: KINTO | Section: Exclusions – Used, Resold, and Improperly Stored Tires
Used or resold tires are not covered.
Tires stored improperly or not used in accordance with the manufacturer’s tire manual are excluded from warranty coverage.

Brand: KINTO | Section: Exclusions – Chemicals, Environment, and External Damage
The KINTO warranty does not cover tires damaged by chemicals, fire, water, floods, accidents, or changes or modifications to the vehicle or suspension from the original configuration.
Damage caused by weather conditions and atmospheric effects is also excluded.

Brand: KINTO | Section: Exclusions – Unauthorized Purchase
The KINTO warranty does not cover tires purchased from stores or agents that are not authorized or approved distributors of KINTO tires.

Brand: KINTO | Section: Road Hazard Limitation and Termination
Warranty coverage for road hazard injuries caused by obstacles or debris is terminated if the tire shows misuse, tread delamination from use, insertion of foreign materials such as waterproofing foam, improper disassembly or assembly, unbalanced tires, defective wheel pans, or operation with improper inflation.
Coverage is also terminated if the vehicle exceeds specified tire load or speed standards or if damage results from neglected vehicle maintenance.

Brand: KINTO | Section: Limitation of Warranty
The KINTO warranty is limited strictly to the terms stated in this policy and does not extend to any conditions outside those expressly covered.
