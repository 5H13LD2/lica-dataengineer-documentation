Brand: Laufenn | Section: Overview
This limited warranty applies to the original purchaser of any new Laufenn tire manufactured by Hankook Tire and Technology and bearing Department of Transportation prescribed tire identification numbers. Eligible Laufenn tires must be used on the vehicle on which they were originally installed and in accordance with the vehicle manufacturer’s or Hankook’s recommendations.

Brand: Laufenn | Section: Eligibility
The Laufenn limited warranty applies only if all of the following conditions are met:

* The tire was purchased on or after January 1, 2023
* The tire size, load rating, and speed rating are equal to or greater than those recommended by the vehicle manufacturer
* The tire has not become unserviceable due to any condition listed under exclusions
* The tire is within six (6) years from the date of manufacture or six (6) years from the date of purchase, whichever is more beneficial to the customer

Brand: Laufenn | Section: Coverage Duration
Laufenn tires are covered for a maximum period of six (6) years, counted either from the date of manufacture or the date of purchase, whichever provides longer coverage to the customer, subject to all warranty terms and exclusions.

Brand: Laufenn | Section: Exclusions – Abuse, Damage, and Improper Use
The Laufenn warranty does not apply to tires that become unserviceable due to willful abuse, collision, wreck, or fire.
Continued use of the tire while flat or under severe underinflation or overinflation is not covered.

Brand: Laufenn | Section: Exclusions – Road Hazards
The Laufenn warranty does not cover road hazard damage, including punctures, cuts, impact breaks, stone drilling, bruises, bulges, snags, or collision-related damage.

Brand: Laufenn | Section: Exclusions – Wear and Mechanical Issues
The Laufenn warranty does not cover premature or irregular wear caused by vehicle mechanical issues or improper maintenance.
This includes conditions resulting from improper mounting or demounting, underinflation, improper tire size, improper repair, vehicle defects, abuse, improper storage, or tire modifications or alterations.

Brand: Laufenn | Section: Exclusions – Ozone, Weather, and Cosmetic Conditions
The Laufenn warranty does not cover cosmetic ozone or weather cracking.
Ozone or weather cracking on tires that are over four (4) years old from the date of manufacture is not covered.

Brand: Laufenn | Section: Exclusions – Ride Disturbance and Tread Limits
The Laufenn warranty does not cover ride disturbance complaints after 2/32 inch of tread wear or one (1) year from the date of purchase, whichever occurs first.
Tires with 2/32 inch or less of remaining tread depth are not covered.

Brand: Laufenn | Section: Exclusions – Identification and Use Restrictions
The Laufenn warranty does not cover tires with serial numbers that have been cut or buffed.
Racing use, off-road use, or tire misapplication is excluded.

Brand: Laufenn | Section: Exclusions – Losses and Commercial Use
The Laufenn warranty does not cover loss of time or use, inconvenience, or any incidental or consequential damages.
Tires used in commercial service are not covered, except for light truck (LT) tires.

Brand: Laufenn | Section: General Limitation
This Laufenn limited warranty applies only to tires that meet all eligibility requirements and excludes all conditions listed above. Coverage is limited strictly to the terms stated in this warranty.
